## Before posting

- Keep "Attorney advertising" in the bio. Tennessee treats social posts about
  your services as advertising under RPC 7.1 to 7.3.
- No client stories, results, or reviews without written consent, and no
  "expert" or "specialist" wording (RPC 7.4).
- Don't answer specific legal questions in comments or DMs; invite people to
  book a call instead.
