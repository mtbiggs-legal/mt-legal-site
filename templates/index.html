Questions

Common questions

Is there a consultation fee?
Yes. You start with a brief call at no cost to see whether your matter is a fit. The full consultation has a fee, quoted when you book and credited toward your retainer if you sign an engagement letter.
Will I work with a paralegal or an associate?
No. When you go to the doctor, you expect to see the doctor, not someone down the hall. The same is true here: {{ partner.name }} handles your matter personally, with the same care from the first call to the last.
Do you offer payment plans?
Ask during your consultation. Fees and any payment terms are always set out in writing before work begins.
Do you only work with clients in Nashville?
Court matters are handled across Middle Tennessee. Crypto, AI, and tokenization work often involves federal law and clients in other states.
Is what I tell you confidential?
Yes. What you share in a consultation is protected, even if you decide not to hire us.
I’ve already been charged. Is it too late?
No, but sooner is better. Reach out before your first court date so bond, evidence, and deadlines can be addressed early.
What should I bring to my consultation?
Any paperwork you have, such as citations, court notices, contracts, or letters, plus key dates. Never send anyone your private keys or seed phrases, including us.
What does a crypto estate plan include?
A will or trust, an inventory of your digital assets, legal authority for your executor, and a secure way for them to reach your assets without exposing keys today.
Can you help my business accept stablecoins?
Yes. We review your payment partners, customer terms, compliance obligations, and tax treatment so you can accept stablecoins with confidence.
