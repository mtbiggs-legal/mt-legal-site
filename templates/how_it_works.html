Getting started
  • Tell us what happened. Fill out the confidential intake form or use the chat. It takes about five minutes.
  • Intro call. A brief call at no cost to see whether your matter is a fit.
  • Consultation. We review your matter, run a conflict check, and talk through options, costs, and timelines in plain English. The consultation fee is quoted when you book and credited toward your retainer if you hire us.
  • Engagement letter. If we’re a fit, you get a written fee agreement before any work begins.
  • Your client portal. Documents, messages, court dates, and billing in one secure, encrypted place.
What to have ready
  • Paperwork. Citations, court notices, contracts, letters, or anything you have received.
  • Dates. Upcoming court dates or deadlines, and when key events happened.
  • For digital assets. Wallet and exchange names, token or project details, and any property involved. Never send private keys or seed phrases.
  • Questions. Write down what you most want to know.
