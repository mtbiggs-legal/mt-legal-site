Why Nashville

Music City is becoming a tech city.

Nashville built its name on music and healthcare. Now some of the largest technology companies in the world are planting roots here, led by Oracle’s plan to make Nashville its world headquarters. Oracle’s move to Nashville

Headquarters

A world headquarters on the East Bank

From a 2021 campus deal to a 2024 headquarters announcement.

  • 2021 campus deal. Oracle announced a major Nashville campus on the East Bank of the Cumberland River, with state and Metro incentives tied to a pledge of 8,500 Nashville jobs by the end of 2031.
  • 2024 headquarters plan. Chairman Larry Ellison announced that Nashville would become Oracle’s world headquarters.
  • Still ramping up. Oracle’s Nashville headcount remains well below the jobs pledge, and its SEC filings still list Austin. The campus is a long-term build-out, not an overnight move.
Campus

The campus plans

About 80 acres, 23 buildings, and a central lake.

  • Scale. Recent reports describe 23 buildings arranged around a central lake across roughly 82 acres on the East Bank.
  • Design. Foster + Partners has been brought on to design the campus.
  • Beyond offices. Plans reported in 2026 include a 120-key Nobu hotel at the south end of the site, and Oracle has sought residential zoning for part of it.
  • Investment. Published estimates range from the original $1.2 billion to more than $4 billion.
Timeline

Where the project stands

Site work first, buildings later.

  • Early 2026. Oracle filed permits to demolish about 515,000 square feet of industrial buildings on the site.
  • Spring 2026. Oracle told city leaders it is moving forward with the East Bank project despite corporate layoffs.
  • Next. A start date for vertical construction has not been announced; the incentive agreement runs through 2031.
East Bank

A whole new district

Oracle anchors a redevelopment reshaping the riverfront.

Oracle’s site sits inside the larger East Bank redevelopment across the river from downtown, where Metro is planning new streets, transit, housing, and the new Titans stadium. For businesses that means new office, retail, and residential space, and the leases, development deals, and contracts that come with it.

Based on public reporting through September 2026. Oracle’s plans, costs, and timelines have changed several times and may change again.

The wider tech hub

Health tech

America’s healthcare capital

Health-care companies and the startups that serve them.

Nashville is home to HCA Healthcare and hundreds of health-care companies, which makes it a natural base for health-tech and health-AI startups. Those companies face some of the strictest privacy and data rules anywhere.

Music & AI

Where AI meets the music industry

The ELVIS Act was written here for a reason.

Tennessee’s ELVIS Act protects artists’ voices and likenesses from AI cloning. Labels, publishers, studios, and music-tech startups in Nashville are at the front line of how AI and creative rights fit together.

Business climate

Tennessee’s advantages

No state income tax on wages and a modern business code.
  • No state income tax on wages or investment income, which draws founders, executives, and investors.
  • Digital-asset friendly entities. Tennessee recognizes decentralized organizations (DAO LLCs) and series LLCs.
  • Talent. Vanderbilt, Belmont, Fisk, Tennessee State, and nearby MTSU produce graduates in law, business, health care, and engineering.
For you

What it means for your business

Growth brings contracts, entities, and new risks.
  • Starting up. Entity formation, founder agreements, and fundraising for companies moving to or launching in Nashville.
  • Selling to big tech. Vendor, licensing, and data agreements with large enterprise customers.
  • AI and data. Privacy, AI governance, and IP for products built here.
  • Real estate and tokenization. East Bank and Nashville property, including fractional ownership through tokenization.
